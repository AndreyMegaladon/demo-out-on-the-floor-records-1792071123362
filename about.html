About Out On The Floor Records

Located at 10 Inverness Street just off Camden High Street, Out On The Floor Records is Camden's oldest surviving independent record shop, having served music lovers and serious vinyl collectors for over three decades. Named after the Northern Soul classic by Dobie Gray, the shop has grown into a legendary destination for crate-diggers from all over the world.

The store is particularly world-renowned for its unrivaled selection of Jamaican music, ska, and reggae, curated by co-owner Jake, who also runs the celebrated Tuff Scout reggae label. Alongside a massive inventory of vintage and original pressing reggae, visitors can explore a diverse, carefully maintained spectrum of genres including 1960s and 1970s rock, punk, metal, soul, and jazz.

Spread across multiple levels—including a legendary basement level housing specialized independent selections like Ra-Ra Rock Records—the shop offers an authentic, traditional crate-digging experience. Staffed by a knowledgeable and passionate team, Out On The Floor remains an essential sanctuary for music enthusiasts seeking rare gems, quality reissues, and music memorabilia in the heart of London.
